Add it to Safari (once)

  1. Tap Copy code above.
  2. In Safari, tap Share → Add Bookmark on any page. Name it RNU login and save.
  3. Tap the Bookmarks button (open book), tap Edit, then tap RNU login. Clear the address and paste the code. Tap Done.

Every time

  1. Open my.rnu.lv in Safari.
  2. Tap Bookmarks → RNU login. The form fills in.
  3. Press Sign in.

Use Safari, because Chrome on iPhone can't run bookmarks like this. Your details are written inside the bookmark and stay on your devices; this page doesn't send or store anything. Don't share the bookmark with anyone.
